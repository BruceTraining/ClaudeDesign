Welcome to Happy Travels!!!

Happy Travels is a full service travel agency located in beautiful San Francisco, California. We are a small family business and we have been planning vacations for our happy customers since 1998. Whether you want a beach, a city, or a mountain, we have the trip for YOU! We book airline tickets, hotels, cruises, rental cars, and complete vacation packages. Our goal is 100% customer satisfaction.

How it works: (1) You tell us where you want to go and how much you want to spend. (2) We plan every detail of the trip. (3) You go on vacation and we stay on call the whole time in case anything goes wrong. It's that easy!

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do you charge a fee?
A: Yes, we charge a $150 planning fee per trip, and the fee is credited toward your trip when you book with us.

Q: Do you book the flights too?
A: Yes! We book your flights, hotels, transfers, tours, and travel insurance, so you have ONE phone number to call.

Q: What if something goes wrong during my trip?
A: Call us. We answer the phone seven days a week while you are traveling, and we fix it.

Q: Can we travel as a group?
A: Yes, we plan trips for groups of up to 12 people, such as families, friends, and clubs.
